# Prince selected-material overlay tiles

## Context

The former four-card treatment placed tall images and their descriptions in
separate blocks, making the selected-materials area visually heavier than the
surrounding portfolio narrative.

## Decision

The four Prince of Egypt material links use shorter square image tiles. Their
number, title, and concise description appear over a darkened lower image area
so each tile remains both visually compact and easy to understand. The small
supporting line beside the section title is reduced slightly in size.

## Options considered

- **Smaller portrait overlays:** retain the former taller image proportion in
  narrower cards.
- **Square overlays (chosen):** use shorter tiles distributed evenly across the
  full content width.

## Trade-offs

- Pros: a calmer, more compact section with material context kept close to its image.
- Cons: overlay copy depends on the image treatment rather than reading as a
  separate text column.

## Revisit if

The material descriptions become long enough that the overlay is no longer
comfortably legible.
