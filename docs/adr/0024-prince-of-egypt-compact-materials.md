# Prince of Egypt compact supporting materials

## Context

The large, full-width stage photograph separated the two related project-process disclosures, and
the selected-material links were visually larger than the compact Object Translations image preview.
The owner selected the image-and-details arrangement from the design lab.

## Decision

Place the stage photograph in a smaller left-hand column with Creative Direction and Production and
Handoffs disclosures beside it. Show the four Selected Materials links as a compact four-across row
of clickable image cards on larger screens, collapsing to two columns on phones.

## Options considered

- **Image above or after disclosures:** preserves a linear reading order but keeps the pieces more
  separated.
- **Image beside disclosures (chosen):** visually groups the stage work with the related process
  notes.

## Trade-offs

- The image is less immersive than a full-width stage photograph.
- Supporting-card descriptions are more compact while their destination links remain unchanged.

## Revisit if

- The project adds more than four selected-material pages.
