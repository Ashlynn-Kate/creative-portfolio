# Role language and project attribution

## Context

The home page, About biography, discipline tags, and case studies used different
labels for Ashlynn's work. They did not make clear that she performs as well as
directs, that she creates every aspect of Object Translations herself, or which
parts of Mindspace and The Prince of Egypt she develops with collaborators.
Ashlynn wants opportunities in creative and artistic direction, choreography,
film, portrait concepts, and performance, but is not seeking commissions to
photograph other people.

## Decision

Use a concise home-page role line naming creative and artistic direction,
choreography, and performance. Keep the About introduction focused on the media
of her work, and integrate performance into her existing stage-experience
sentence; do not tie the biography to Mindspace's temporary production status. Credit
Object Translations as an entirely self-created series, including photography
and appearing in the portraits. Credit Mindspace's original concept and lead
performance to Ashlynn, and its direction and choreography as shared with
Breana. Credit The Prince of Egypt's story, casting, music selection and edits,
costumes, and choreography to Ashlynn, while describing set and lighting work
as collaboration with their teams. Keep the existing Photography browse tag
unchanged for the separate category-language review.

## Options considered

- **One broad “multidisciplinary artist” label:** concise but obscures roles
  relevant to employers and collaborators.
- **List every skill as a site-wide service:** captures breadth but implies
  photography of others is an offered service and hides project ownership.
- **Name primary roles site-wide and credit contributions per project (chosen):**
  establishes a clear professional identity without flattening collaboration.

## Trade-offs

- The short home-page line cannot explain every activity; case studies provide
  the necessary detail.
- Specific project credits need review as collaborators and production status
  change.

## Revisit if

- Ashlynn begins offering photography commissions or changes her primary
  professional roles.
