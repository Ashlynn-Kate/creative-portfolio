# Object Translations concepts begin as disclosures

## Context

As the series grows, showing every concept in full makes it difficult to see the names
and choose what to explore without extensive scrolling.

## Decision

Use a list of closed concept disclosures on both the main Object Translations case study
and the all-concepts page. The concept's title is the disclosure label; its descriptive
keywords remain visible beside the title on larger screens.

## Options considered

- **Show every concept in full:** makes the visual work immediately visible, but obscures
  the collection's overall shape.
- **Named disclosures (chosen):** gives visitors a quick index and lets them open only
  the concepts that interest them.

## Trade-offs

- A visitor takes one additional click to see each concept's images.
- The page is calmer, shorter, and easier to scan.

## Revisit if

- The collection needs visual thumbnails in the closed state.
