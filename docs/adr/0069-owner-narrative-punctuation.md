# Owner narrative punctuation

## Context
While reviewing Knock on Wood, Ashlynn requested removal of dashes and explained
that she does not like them in her grammar.

## Decision
Replace the explanatory dash in the current paragraph with a comma and retain
this preference when writing or revising narrative copy. This request does not
trigger an unrelated site-wide rewrite. Hyphens within compound words and
non-prose interface marks are unaffected.

## Options considered
- Retain an explanatory dash.
- Use conventional sentence punctuation matching the owner's voice (chosen).

## Trade-offs
- Some sentences may need rephrasing to retain a natural rhythm.

## Revisit if
Ashlynn chooses a different punctuation style.
