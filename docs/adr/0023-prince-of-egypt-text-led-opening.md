# Prince of Egypt text-led opening

## Context

The opening promotional image competed with the case study’s Project Overview and separated the
creator role from the explanatory context. The owner chose a calmer text-led beginning in the
design lab.

## Decision

Start The Prince of Egypt with the Project Overview heading and paragraph, then show My Role as a
short labeled note directly beneath it. Remove the promotional image that previously appeared
before the overview; the palace production photograph remains as the first image after the opening text.

## Options considered

- **Small promotional image with role beside it:** retains the image but gives the opening a more
  complex, denser structure.
- **Text-led overview and role (chosen):** makes the project context and responsibility immediately
  readable.

## Trade-offs

- The case study opens less visually than before.
- The production imagery has more breathing room after the introductory context.

## Revisit if

- A new hero image becomes important enough to lead the story without competing with the overview.
