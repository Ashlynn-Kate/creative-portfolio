# Choreographic work detail rhythm

## Context

The initial Knock on Wood page used the generic full-width video treatment and
centered text-column placement. Ashlynn wanted the performance still to be
more compact and the work’s narrative to align directly beneath it.

## Decision

Choreographic Works detail pages use a 50%-width, uncropped landscape video
thumbnail on desktop and full width on mobile. Their role, headings, and prose
begin at the wide column’s left edge, with reduced flow spacing.

## Options considered

- **Keep the generic full-width video:** gives the media strongest emphasis.
- **Compact half-width media (chosen):** prioritizes the writing and creates a
  more balanced editorial page.

## Trade-offs

- Pros: clearer visual hierarchy and a consistent left edge for the content.
- Cons: the performance image has less visual impact on large screens.

## Revisit if

A future choreographic work needs an image-led or full-performance-page
treatment.
