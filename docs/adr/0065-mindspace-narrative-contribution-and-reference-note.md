# Mindspace narrative contribution and reference note

## Context
Ashlynn preferred the narrative and quiet-credit treatments used elsewhere to the labelled
role box. She approved the proposed first-person sentence and requested image attribution clarity.

## Decision
Replace the role block with a narrative paragraph retaining accurate shared direction and
choreography, original concept, lead performance, and production coordination.
This is consistent with ADR-0030's placement directly after Concept, not a change to it.
Place the inspiration note below the visual-reference carousel in small muted text.
Keep the earlier Creative Process removal and unnumbered heading of ADR-0061.

## Options considered
- Quiet credits like Gift of Athens.
- Behind the work heading like individual dance pieces.
- First-person contribution in the concept narrative (chosen).

## Trade-offs
- The contribution reads naturally without a separate label.
- The reference note distinguishes inspiration from original portfolio work.

## Revisit if
Final film imagery replaces the reference collection.
