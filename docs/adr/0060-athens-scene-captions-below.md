# Gift of Athens scene captions below thumbnails

## Context
The owner compared the Death of Socrates title overlay with the other scene-video captions
and approved moving it below the image for legibility and consistency. This supersedes
ADR-0058's Death of Socrates overlay choice.

## Decision
Keep every other decision in ADR-0058: illustrated introduction, uniform chapter index,
unnumbered visible Socrates/Plato/Aristotle chapters, then Creating the production and
Giving back to the community; the left-aligned wide headings, compact rehearsal footage
with wrapping copy, and uncropped community photographs with the award-ceremony caption.
Retain the existing thumbnails, Drive destinations, and compact 16:9 scene-video presentation.
Show Death of Socrates below the Socrates–Plato duet image using the same caption treatment
as the other scene clips. Keep the play button and Watch the clip cue on the image.

## Options considered
- Title on the thumbnail: compact, but covers the photograph and differs from the other clips.
- Title below the thumbnail (chosen): consistent and easier to read.

## Trade-offs
- Adds a short caption line while leaving the photograph unobstructed.

## Revisit if
- The owner chooses a different treatment for all scene videos.
