# Choreographic work video narrative

## Context

The first choreographic-work page, Knock on Wood, no longer needs a separate
role statement or repeated introductory sentence. Its context is best served
by placing the opening narrative directly beside the video thumbnail.

## Decision

Choreographic Works pages pair the first performance-video thumbnail with the
Behind the work heading and copy. The uncropped thumbnail floats on the left
at half the wide content area on desktop; the narrative begins beside it and
continues below it if needed. On mobile, the thumbnail and narrative stack.

## Options considered

- **Separate video and narrative blocks:** makes each element more isolated.
- **Wrapped video narrative (chosen):** creates a more editorial, compact
  opening while keeping the performance and its context together.

## Trade-offs

- Pros: removes duplicated context and gives the opening a clear visual flow.
- Cons: long copy is less uniform than a single-width text column.

## Revisit if

A future work needs a feature-length video or a substantially longer opening
narrative.
