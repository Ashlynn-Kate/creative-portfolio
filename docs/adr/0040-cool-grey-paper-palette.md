# Cool grey paper palette

## Context

The portfolio's original warm cream paper gave the editorial design a soft,
warm foundation. The owner selected a neutral cool-grey alternative that keeps
the same light visual intensity while shifting the site's atmosphere.

## Decision

Use Option A's airy cool-grey palette: `#eef0f2` for paper, `#e2e5e8` for
recessed paper surfaces, and `#cbd0d5` for rules. The home hero's photographic
wash and feathered edges use the same paper color, so image boundaries continue
to dissolve into the page.

## Options considered

- **Airy cool grey (chosen):** closest to the original paper's lightness while
  clearly neutral and cool.
- **Soft slate grey:** a more pronounced cool tone, but slightly darker than
  the requested intensity.

## Trade-offs

- Pros: preserves the site's light, editorial openness with a cooler mood.
- Cons: the warmer accent and imagery contrast more visibly against the new
  neutral paper.

## Revisit if

The owner wants a darker site mood or the accent palette changes substantially.
