# Mindspace role after concept

## Context

The wide-case narrative alignment placed Mindspace’s role in its header.
Ashlynn decided the role reads more clearly as a supporting project detail
after the Concept paragraph, matching the text-led Prince of Egypt opening.

## Decision

Prince of Egypt and Mindspace retain the wide narrative alignment established
for the home page. Mindspace’s role is rendered beneath its Concept paragraph,
at the same left edge as the other narrative blocks.

## Options considered

- **Role with header:** keeps the role beneath the discipline tags.
- **Role after concept (chosen):** lets visitors understand the work before
  reading Ashlynn’s contribution.

## Trade-offs

- Pros: creates a consistent text-led opening with Prince of Egypt.
- Cons: the role is no longer immediately visible beside the title.

## Revisit if

Mindspace gains more header metadata that needs a separate compact role area.
