# Gift of Athens story-first order and community photographs

## Context
The owner found the production-process section interrupted the promise of “Follow the ideas.”
She chose community lab option D, with a slightly smaller vertical photograph and a caption
identifying the award ceremony with the mayor and local veterans group. She approved these
changes after discussing them. This supersedes ADR-0057's section order and community grid.

## Decision
- Keep the illustrated two-column introduction, unlabeled credits, uniform image-led chapter
  index, and visible philosopher chapters from ADR-0057, without chapter numbers.
- Place the three story chapters directly after the index, then Creating the production,
  then Giving back to the community. Preserve “Beyond appearance” as Plato's title.
- Retain the compact rehearsal thumbnail and its wrapping copy, the “Story to stage |
  Rehearsal footage” subtitle, and the desktop continuation beneath the thumbnail at
  “periodically.” Keep the standalone opening free of the upper divider and excess space.
- Retain compact 16:9 Google Drive scene links with the existing chapter thumbnails and captions,
  including the Death of Socrates identifying caption on its Socrates–Plato duet thumbnail.
- Align production and community headings and subheads with their wide narrative column.
- Build community option D with an uncropped dedication image at the opening's left corner,
  a moderate text gap, and wrapping prose. Keep the uncropped flag/veterans image on the right,
  reducing its desktop maximum width from 230px to 210px and its relative width from 25% to 23%.
- Caption the veterans image “Award ceremony with the mayor and local veterans group.”
  Stack the photographs and text on phones to preserve a comfortable reading width.
- Keep this production listed under Live performance → Full-length productions.
- Store the images and prose in a typed image-story block rendered with MediaImage, consistent
  with ADR-0005 and ADR-0007.

## Options considered
- Keep process before the chapters: interrupted the narrative journey.
- Community image grid: separated the images from the story and gave the dedication too much space.
- Story first and community option D (chosen): preserves the story sequence and relates images to prose.

## Trade-offs
- The story is easier to follow and the community photographs retain their complete proportions.
- The page remains long; compact thumbnails and the chapter index provide navigation and visual breaks.
- Mobile stacks the photographs rather than squeezing the prose between two images.

## Revisit if
- Additional chapters or community resources need more space.
