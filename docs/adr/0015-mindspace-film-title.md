# Mindspace is the title of the dance film

## Context

The work is set to an acoustic cover of Gnarls Barkley's “Crazy,” but the song title
does not represent the quieter, introspective, and gently uncanny experience of the film.

## Decision

The portfolio names the work *Mindspace*. “Dance film” is used as its short descriptive
label, without foregrounding the song title in the project copy.

## Options considered

- **Dance Film:** descriptive, but not a distinctive title.
- **Empty Space / The Space Between:** evocative alternatives.
- **Mindspace (chosen):** concise and centered on the film's inner-world setting.

## Trade-offs

- The title communicates atmosphere rather than the song reference.
- Existing references to the work need to use the new title consistently.

## Revisit if

- A different final title is selected before the film is released.
