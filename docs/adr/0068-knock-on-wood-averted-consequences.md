# Knock on Wood averted consequences

## Context
Ashlynn wanted unfamiliar viewers to understand the superstition underlying the
piece. She clarified that a second character approaches the main character until
he performs the protective gesture, avoiding the consequences, and approved the copy.

## Decision
Use two paragraphs under Behind the work: first explain the expression and gesture,
then describe the approaching character and the narrowly avoided threat.
Retain retrogradation in Choreographic approach but connect reversal to the
turning point rather than an actual reversal of good fortune into misfortune.
Align the summary with that ending. Keep the presentation details unchanged.
This is consistent with ADR-0032; the existing video narrative layout is unchanged.

## Options considered
- A brief reference to superstition and reversal of fortune.
- Explain the ritual, approaching threat, and avoided consequence (chosen).

## Trade-offs
- The added context makes the narrative legible to viewers unfamiliar with the phrase.
- Longer copy wraps around the existing video thumbnail.

## Revisit if
Ashlynn further clarifies the choreography's relationship to the story.
