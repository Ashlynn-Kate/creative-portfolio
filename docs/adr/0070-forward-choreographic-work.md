# Forward choreographic work

## Context
Ashlynn approved a description of Forward's persistent momentum, knee crawl,
expanding and contracting ensemble, and exhausted but triumphant ending.
She supplied the video link and performance photograph and directed publication.

## Decision
Add Forward after Knock on Wood in the individual-piece listings.
Use the existing video-feature block, consistent with ADR-0032, followed by
Choreographic approach and Presentation details. Keep the provided photograph
uncropped and process it through the existing image pipeline.
Label the linked Drive clip Abbreviated performance video.
Credit choreography to Ashlynn Kate and identify the freshman and sophomore
senior-company dancers of GO! Contemporary Dance Works, based at Studio Arts
for Dancers in Knoxville, Tennessee. The credit identifies creative contributions
without making a claim of exclusive copyright ownership.
Keep the approved narrative wording and punctuation preference of ADR-0069.

## Options considered
- A separate design for the new piece.
- Reuse the established individual-work layout (chosen).

## Trade-offs
- A consistent page structure makes additional choreography easy to browse and maintain.

## Revisit if
Ashlynn supplies revised media, credits, or narrative details.
